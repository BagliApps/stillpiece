Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/BagliApps/stillpiece/issues. Please include your device model and system version.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress when I update?
No. Progress is kept on your device across updates.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
On iPhone and iPad, progress syncs through iCloud when you're signed in with the same Apple ID. On Android, it comes back with Google's device backup or when you transfer data to the new phone.

I bought Premium on another device.
Use "Restore Purchases" in the Premium screen with the same Apple or Google account.

Where do the artworks come from?
From public-domain collections: paintings from the Cleveland Museum of Art (CC0), nature and historical photos from the US National Park Service and the Library of Congress. No AI-generated images.
