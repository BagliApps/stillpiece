Privacy Policy

Stillpiece · Effective October 15, 2026

Stillpiece is a jigsaw puzzle game. We built it to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

What we collect

Only anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off, and anonymous usage statistics about the puzzles themselves. The app has no accounts and nothing it sends is linked to you. Free players can choose to watch an optional short ad (see Ads below); there are no ads without your choice and none in Premium. Besides these, it connects to the internet only to download puzzle paintings.

Ads

When the free puzzles of a level run out, free players can choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock one more puzzle. Ads are never shown unprompted and are never shown to Premium subscribers. They are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location to choose and measure ads. On iOS the app asks for your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency); in the EU and UK it shows Google's consent form first. You can decline and still watch ads, but they will be less relevant. You can reset the advertising identifier in your device settings. If an ad bothers you, use Settings › Report an ad problem.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the device model and system version, and a random identifier that lets us count how many devices are affected. It does not contain your name, your photos, your puzzles or any account. We do not store IP addresses, and reports are deleted within 90 days.

You can turn crash reports off in Settings › Data › Crash reports. When they are off, nothing is sent.

Usage statistics

To learn which levels and kinds of pictures people enjoy, the app counts a few events: a puzzle was started, finished or left unfinished, and a finished picture was liked with the heart. Each event says which level and picture it was, how many pieces were placed, how long it took, whether tray or table mode, calm mode or Premium was on, the device's operating system and the app's language. It does not contain your name, your photos, your progress history or any account. Events go to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built not to identify people: the app sends a random number made on your device, scrambled (hashed) so it cannot be traced back, and TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses. If you are offline, events wait on your device (at most a few hundred) and are sent later.

Painting downloads

The puzzle paintings are public-domain works from the Cleveland Museum of Art, plus nature and historical photos from the US National Park Service and the Library of Congress. The app downloads them from GitHub (GitHub Pages, with jsDelivr as a backup) and keeps them on your device. These are plain file downloads: the app sends nothing about you, and only knows which paintings to fetch. As with any website, GitHub or jsDelivr can see your IP address when a file is downloaded; we do not receive it. Downloaded paintings are not included in iCloud or Google backups.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, statistics, streaks, settings and unfinished puzzles.
  • If you make a puzzle from your own photo: the cropped copy of the most recent photo. Older ones are deleted.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your device.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to make a puzzle from your own photo. It reads only the photo you pick or take, crops it and keeps the result on your device. Photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Sync and backup

  • iPhone and iPad: your progress (not your photos) is synced between your devices through Apple's iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We cannot access it. You can turn this off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • Android: Android's system backup can save your progress and your latest puzzle photo to your Google account and move them to a new phone. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Purchases

Any purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To know whether your Premium subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat: it receives a random ID made by the app (not linked to your name or account), your purchase history from the store and your device's operating system and country. We use it only to unlock Premium and restore it on your other devices.

Children

Stillpiece does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Crash reports and usage statistics are anonymous; ads are optional.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the date above before that version is released.
